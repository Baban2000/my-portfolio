// About

Backend-minded, product-focused.

I work as a software developer at Infosolz Consultancy Services, where most of my time goes into backend development with PHP and CodeIgniter 3. Day to day that means implementing application features, writing the business logic behind them, handling database operations in MySQL, and connecting applications to third-party APIs.

Much of my work has been in education technology: campus-management modules, attendance and subject-wise reporting, structured data uploads, administrative tools, and AI-assisted features such as question and lesson-plan generation built on the Gemini and OpenAI APIs.

I enjoy untangling practical problems, improving applications that already exist, and getting steadily better at the craft, whether that is cleaner SQL, better version-control habits, or a new framework.
